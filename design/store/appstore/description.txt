Build your town by day. Defend it by night.

You are the young heir to a fallen kingdom. The Warlord took the throne, and his raiders burn every village they reach. Start with a small wooden fort and 3 villagers, and win the kingdom back one town at a time.

BUILD BY DAY
• Place houses, fields, lumber camps, quarries and markets, and watch your villagers walk to work, chop trees and carry stone home.
• Lay paths so everyone walks faster, and set out flowers, statues and wells to make your town richer.
• Upgrade every building through 3 tiers, from a muddy hovel to a stone townhouse, and your palisade fort to a great castle with blue towers.

DEFEND BY NIGHT
• When the horn sounds, raiders come out of the dark: spearmen, swordsmen, archers, crossbowmen, horsemen and siege engines.
• Build archer towers, crossbow towers, ballistas and catapults, and your villagers climb up to man them.
• Train soldiers and riders at the barracks and stables, and rally them where the fight is hardest.
• Close the gaps with walls and gates, call an arrow volley onto the enemy, and send out your castle's riders.

A FULL CAMPAIGN
• 12 stages, each a different land: green valleys, rivers, dry hills, desert, deep woods, marshes and open plains.
• Each stage brings in new buildings, so you learn them one at a time.
• 2 boss battles against the Raider Chief and the Warlord himself.
• 3 bonus stages on Hard for when the campaign is won: drought, crop blight and deep winter.
• Win stars, then spend them on 36 upgrades for your buildings and your castle.
• Earn 28 achievements and fill the Archive with every building and raider you meet.

MADE FOR PHONE AND TABLET
• Plays upright or sideways on iPhone and iPad.
• Plays offline. No ads, no accounts, no data collected.
• Pick your own colours for your town and for the raiders.
